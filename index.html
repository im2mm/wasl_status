WASL Status — PILOT Extension

This is the hosting folder of the wasl_status PILOT Extension. It lists the vehicles of a PILOT account with their WASL time, WASL state and WASL response.

Entry file: Module.js (defines Store.wasl_status.Module)

Backend: api/wasl (POST, used for "Another account")

Register in PILOT Admin: Name wasl_status, URL = this folder's address.

Documentation: doc/
